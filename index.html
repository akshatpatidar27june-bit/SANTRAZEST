About the initiative

Technology with the orchard at its heart

SANTRAZEST is an agricultural technology initiative focused on making fruit counting and orchard information easier to understand and use.

🌳

Our purpose

Help growers make more informed orchard decisions with accessible digital tools.

🍊

Our focus

Start with orange detection and counting, then develop practical harvest and orchard records.

🤝

Our approach

Keep the experience clear, useful and farmer-first, with technology working quietly in the background.

A simple process

From a tree to a clearer picture

The intended workflow is designed around three straightforward steps.

1

Capture

Use a compatible phone camera to capture an orange tree or select an orchard image.

2

Detect

On-device AI analyses the image and identifies visible fruit for review.

3

Review results

See the estimated count, check the result and save it to your orchard records.

Technology & features

Useful insights, not complicated screens

The platform is planned to bring essential orchard information into one simple place.

  • AI-assisted orange detection and counting
  • Tree-wise and orchard-wise records
  • Harvest quantity estimates where data supports them
  • Indicative value calculations using grower-provided rates
  • Previous detection reports and comparisons
  • Edge AI architecture for on-device processing

Edge AI approach

AI designed to work on the device

SANTRAZEST is planned around local AI inference on compatible devices. This approach aims to reduce dependence on a constant cloud connection for the detection step.

  • Local image processing on supported devices
  • Model version management
  • Clear handling of offline and online states
  • Performance testing across supported phones

Frequently asked questions

Common questions

A quick introduction to the platform's planned capabilities.

What is SANTRAZEST?

SANTRAZEST is an agricultural technology initiative focused on AI-assisted orange counting and orchard insights.

Does it count every orange on a tree?

The system will estimate visible fruit in captured views. Fruit hidden behind leaves or other fruit may not be detected. A complete tree count may require multiple views and careful review.

Does detection require internet?

The planned detection approach uses Edge AI on compatible devices. Some account, synchronization and model update features may still need internet access.

Are weight and income figures guaranteed?

No. These are intended as estimates and will depend on measured or grower-provided inputs such as average fruit weight and selling rate.

Is the farmer app available now?

The platform is under development. Features will be marked available only after implementation and testing.
